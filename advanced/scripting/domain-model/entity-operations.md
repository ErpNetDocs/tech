# Entity operations in scripts

The current guide to retrieving, creating, updating, and deleting Domain entities from JavaScript is [Entity operations](https://docs.erp.net/dev/scripting/domain-model/entity-operations.html) in the Developer Documentation.

Use its [execution-context guide](https://docs.erp.net/dev/scripting/execution-context.html) to distinguish a business rule's `subject` from managed-script inputs.
